# Code of Conduct

MLForm is built in public. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the code, behavior, evidence, and tradeoffs. Assume good intent, but accept correction when your words or actions harm someone.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving actionable feedback and explaining the reasoning behind it.
- Respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and points of view.
- Correcting mistakes openly and helping others do the same.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Abusing project channels for spam, promotion, or unrelated disputes.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository and other official MLForm spaces. It also applies when someone represents MLForm publicly, such as through an official account or event.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [pablo.ulloa.santin@udc.es](mailto:pablo.ulloa.santin@udc.es). Do not open a public issue. Include relevant links, screenshots, or context when safe to do so.

The maintainer will review reports as privately and promptly as practical. Necessary action may include removing content, issuing a warning, limiting participation, or permanently excluding someone from project spaces. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
